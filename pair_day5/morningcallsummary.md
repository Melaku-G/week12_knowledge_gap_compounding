# Morning Call Summary

During the morning call, the original question focused broadly on why benchmark improvements failed to guarantee deployment reliability. Through discussion, the question was sharpened to focus specifically on the interaction between statistical rigor and benchmark distribution coverage.

The conversation clarified that the issue was not a failure of bootstrap statistics themselves, but a limitation of what the benchmark distribution represented. We refined the wording to explicitly distinguish between:
- statistical validity under a sampled distribution,
and:
- behavioral reliability under deployment shift.

The final version centered on how benchmarks can be internally rigorous while remaining externally incomplete.
