# Evening Call Summary

The evening discussion focused on clarifying the distinction between:
- statistical rigor,
and:
- deployment completeness.

Feedback emphasized that the benchmark itself was not “wrong”; rather, its guarantees were conditional on the benchmark distribution remaining representative. Based on that discussion, the explainer was revised to focus more explicitly on:
- subgroup coverage,
- distribution shift,
- and benchmark blind spots.

The final version sharpened the central insight that statistically rigorous evaluation can still fail operationally when deployment behavior diverges from benchmark assumptions.
